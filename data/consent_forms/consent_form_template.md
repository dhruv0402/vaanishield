## What we are asking you to do
We will record you speaking naturally in a mix of English and [regional language], including
sentences that switch between the two languages mid-sentence. We will then use AI
voice-cloning software to generate synthetic versions of your voice speaking the same or
similar sentences.

## How this will be used
- Both your original recordings and the AI-generated clones will be used to train and test a
  research system that tries to detect AI-cloned voices.
- Anonymized or aggregated results (e.g. accuracy numbers) may appear in an academic
  submission, paper, or presentation.
- Short audio clips (yours and/or the cloned version) may be played during a demo or
  presentation of this project, [with / without] identifying you by name — *(delete as
  applicable and confirm with participant)*.

## What we will NOT do
- We will not use your voice or its clone for any purpose other than this research project
  without asking you again.
- We will not use your voice clone to impersonate you in any real communication.
- We will not share your raw recordings outside the project team.

## Your rights
- You may withdraw consent at any time; if you do, your recordings and any clones generated
  from them will be deleted.
- You may ask to review how your voice was used before any public demo or submission.
